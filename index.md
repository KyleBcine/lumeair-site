# LumeAir Home

<p class="gh-only">You're reading the source of this page on GitHub. <a href="https://kylebcine.github.io/lumeair-site/"><strong>Open it on the LumeAir website</strong></a>.</p>

Know what you're breathing at home. LumeAir sensors measure the air in each room, and the LumeAir Home app shows you every reading on your phone.

Your readings stay at home. The app talks to your sensors directly over your Wi-Fi: there are no accounts and no LumeAir servers.

## What it measures

- **CO₂**
- **Particles**, PM1.0 to PM10
- **VOC and NOx** indexes
- **Temperature and humidity**
- **Air pressure**

LumeAir also works with [Home Assistant](Support.md#home-assistant), and can send alerts to your phone when a reading gets high.
